## Ethan full source prompt

Make an ORIGINAL transparent animation SOURCE BOARD for ONLY ETHAN, the ivory-and-gold young adult male in the attached target pose board. Preserve that exact identity: short dark brown hair, natural three-quarter face (small shaded nose, no protruding beak), broad shoulders, long ivory split coat with intricate gold trim, dark fitted leather layers/belts/boots, golden rune forearm with cyan core. All poses face RIGHT; consistent adult anatomy and head size across poses. Do not use simplified polygons. Rich clean material detail as in reference. Exactly FOUR ROWS separated by wide transparent gutters, no text/labels/grid/shadows. ROW 1 exactly THREE upright standing-jump phases: rising with knees pulled up, apex tuck, descending with legs extending downward. ROW 2 exactly THREE running-jump phases: strong forward lean INCLUDING TORSO, knees/coat trail back on takeoff, forward airborne apex, extending a landing leg. ROW 3 exactly TWO landing poses: deep clear crouch with bent knees and lowered head/torso, then half-rise recovery. ROW 4 exactly FOUR rune attack poses: very brief ready with forearm partially raised, arm extended firing toward right, recoil, recovery toward idle. No projectile flashes or detached effects; rune core may be cyan. Keep every entire figure and boots in frame, all figures separated and no overlap. Use the original target poses as anchor silhouettes. Large clear source figures, transparent background; final native 136px sprites will be separately resampled, palette-cleaned and hand-touched.

ROW 5 exactly FOUR one-swing sword attack poses: quick ready with sword lifted, decisive forward cut, low follow-through, recovery. Use target poses as silhouette/appearance anchors. Transparent background. These large source poses will later be resampled/cleaned to native 128px sprites.

## Processing and hand cleanup

Reproduce with python next/tools/build-full-x2.py from the repository root. Segment connected opaque figures, remove detached source marks/glow, fit each action at its own height (retain landing crouch), straighten standing-jump torsos, align head/feet to native pivots. Reduce all runtime PNGs to one 64-color palette, binary alpha, native one-pixel contour highlights/shadows, remove isolated specks, close isolated glove/cloth holes. Existing selected idle/run faces retain the prior manual pixel cleanup. Running jumps retain torso lean. Attack hand anchors are measured per-frame; prep lasts two ticks. Rune loops; three thin curved slash phases play once after prep, forward from the sword hand, mirrored with the attacker. Tiles/background/effects are drawn at native scale 2 by the builder, not enlarged scale-1 exports. Tiles have 60px cells; ledge opacity ends at row31. No changes to simulation values.

Generated boards are source material, not proof of likeness. Target-versus-sprite pose comparisons and actual attack captures are in ../pixel-review/. Likeness and motion quality await user review.
